### 🎙️ SpeakNova AI — AI Spoken-English Coach `Personal project · Live on Google Play · 5,000+ installs`

Built and shipped **solo**: an AI spoken-English coach for Hindi-first Indian learners — Android app plus a companion web client on one account.

- Text-chat and **voice practice** (speech-to-text, text-to-speech, streamed replies) on the **OpenAI API**, proxied through a **Node.js / Express + Laravel** layer so keys never touch the client
- Instant grammar and pronunciation corrections, conversation history in **MongoDB**
- Passwordless **email OTP + Google OAuth**, **Zustand** state, level-based onboarding, streaks and progress
- Subscription flow with a ₹9 three-day trial auto-renewing at ₹249/month

`React Native` `TypeScript` `OpenAI API` `Node.js` `Express` `Laravel` `MongoDB` `Zustand`

`React Native` `TypeScript` `Redux Toolkit` `Google Maps API` `Firebase` `Stripe`
